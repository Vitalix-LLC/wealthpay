Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

WealthPay ("WealthPay", "we", "us" or "our") provides payment processing and payment technology services to businesses. Protecting personal information is part of doing that job properly, and this Privacy Policy describes the information we handle, why we handle it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights available to you.

Please read this policy carefully. By using our website or our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices described here. If you do not agree with them, please do not use our website or services.

In short: we collect the information we need to process payments, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and improve our services. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use card data for advertising.

2. Who we are

The entity responsible for the processing of personal information described in this policy is:

CompanyWealthPay
Websitewealthpay.io

Depending on the situation, WealthPay may act as a controller, when we decide why and how personal information is processed, or as a processor, when we process information on behalf of a merchant that uses our services. Where we act as a processor, the merchant's own privacy policy governs the relationship with the customer, and requests about that data should be directed to the merchant. We will support the merchant in responding.

3. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information we process about:

  • Visitors of our website and anyone who contacts us through our channels.
  • Merchants and their representatives, including account applicants, administrators and beneficial owners.
  • End customers who make a payment to a merchant using WealthPay as the payment processor.
  • Partners, suppliers and candidates who interact with us in a business context.

This policy does not apply to the products, websites or practices of merchants, partners or any other third party, even when you reach them through a link on our website.

4. Information we collect

4.1 Information you provide to us

  • Identification and contact data: full name, email address, telephone number, postal address, date of birth, nationality.
  • Account and onboarding data: business name, tax or registration numbers, trade activity, expected volume, banking details for settlement, ownership structure and identification documents required by law.
  • Verification data: government-issued identification, proof of address, and, where the verification method requires it, a selfie or liveness check used solely to confirm identity.
  • Transaction data: payment amount, currency, method, timestamp, descriptors, order references and any information you submit with the transaction.
  • Support and communications: the content of messages, forms, emails and calls, including recordings when we notify you that a call is recorded.

4.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, language, time zone, device identifiers and screen characteristics.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, referring pages, session duration and interactions with our dashboard or checkout.
  • Fraud prevention signals: device fingerprinting, behavioral patterns during checkout, velocity of attempts and geolocation inferred from IP address.
  • Cookie data: identifiers stored in cookies and similar technologies, as described in section 8.

4.3 Information from third parties

  • Financial institutions, acquirers and card networks: authorization results, chargeback and dispute records, and settlement information.
  • Identity, credit and sanctions databases: results of know-your-customer, anti-money-laundering, sanctions and politically-exposed-person screening.
  • Fraud prevention networks: risk indicators associated with a card, device, email address or IP address.
  • Merchants: data about a payment or a customer necessary to process, refund or dispute a transaction.
  • Public sources and business partners: corporate registries, public filings and referral information.

5. Payment and card data

When a payment is made through WealthPay, card credentials are captured in a PCI DSS Level 1 environment. The card number is encrypted at the point of entry and replaced by a token. Merchants receive the token and a masked reference, not the full card number.

  • We do not store the card verification code (CVV/CVC) after authorization.
  • We never use card data for marketing, profiling for advertising, or any purpose unrelated to processing the payment, preventing fraud or complying with the law.
  • Card data may be retained in tokenized form to enable recurring charges, refunds, one-click payments and dispute handling, where the transaction or the merchant's instruction requires it.
  • Access to the vault is restricted, logged and reviewed, and is granted only to personnel whose role requires it.

6. How we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service: to authorize, capture, route, refund and settle payments, and to make our dashboard, APIs and support channels work.
  • Onboarding and underwriting: to assess whether an applicant meets our risk, regulatory and contractual requirements.
  • Fraud, risk and security: to detect, investigate and prevent fraudulent transactions, account takeover, money laundering and abuse of our systems.
  • Legal and regulatory compliance: to meet obligations related to anti-money laundering, sanctions, tax, accounting, card network rules and lawful requests from authorities.
  • Communication: to respond to requests, send service notices, transaction alerts, security warnings and changes to our terms.
  • Improvement and analytics: to understand how our products are used, diagnose problems, measure performance and develop new features.
  • Marketing: to send information about products and offers to business contacts, always with an option to opt out.
  • Protecting rights: to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, and to enforce our agreements.

We may aggregate or anonymize information so that it no longer identifies anyone, and use it for statistics, benchmarking and research. Anonymized data is not subject to this policy.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels and software development kits. We group them as follows:

  • Strictly necessary: required for authentication, session integrity, load balancing, checkout security and fraud detection. These cannot be disabled without breaking the service.
  • Functional: remember preferences such as language, region and dashboard layout.
  • Analytics: help us understand traffic, feature usage and performance so we can improve the product.
  • Marketing: measure the effectiveness of campaigns and may allow partners to show relevant content on other websites.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, and where required by law we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of our website and checkout behave. Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Since there is no common standard for interpreting it, we do not respond to it at this time.

9. How we share information

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in the sense given to those terms by applicable privacy laws. We share information only in the following situations:

  • Financial partners: acquirers, issuers, card networks, banks and payment method providers, as necessary to process, settle or dispute a transaction.
  • Merchants: when you pay a business that uses WealthPay, we share the transaction data that business needs to fulfil your order and manage refunds or disputes.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, identity verification, fraud prevention, customer support, communications and analytics vendors, bound by contract to process data only on our instructions.
  • Regulators and authorities: when required by law, regulation, court order or a valid request from a competent authority.
  • Professional advisers: auditors, lawyers, accountants and insurers, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Corporate transactions: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, in which case the recipient remains bound by this policy until it is lawfully replaced.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you have asked or agreed that we share the information.

10. International transfers

WealthPay operates internationally, and personal information may be transferred to, stored in or accessed from countries other than the one where it was collected, including the United States. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws of your country.

When we transfer personal information across borders, we apply appropriate safeguards, which may include Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, equivalent clauses approved by other authorities, adequacy decisions, or the international transfer mechanisms recognized by the LGPD. You may request information about the safeguards we use for a specific transfer.

11. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected, and for the periods required by law. In practice this means:

CategoryTypical retention period
Transaction and settlement recordsAt least 5 years after the transaction, to meet financial and anti-money-laundering obligations.
Onboarding and verification documentsDuration of the relationship plus the retention period required by applicable regulation, generally 5 years.
Tokenized card credentialsUntil the merchant or the cardholder requests deletion, or the token is no longer needed for recurring charges, refunds or disputes.
Support communicationsUp to 3 years after the last interaction.
Website analytics and cookie dataUp to 24 months, or less where the cookie expires earlier.

When a retention period ends, we delete the information or anonymize it irreversibly. Records subject to a legal hold or an ongoing dispute are kept until the matter is resolved.

12. How we protect information

We maintain technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These include:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS, and encryption at rest for sensitive data stores.
  • Field-level encryption and tokenization of card credentials.
  • Role-based access control, least-privilege provisioning and multi-factor authentication for internal systems.
  • Network segmentation, continuous monitoring, logging and intrusion detection.
  • Annual PCI DSS assessment, penetration testing and vulnerability management.
  • Security training for personnel and confidentiality obligations for anyone who handles data.
  • Incident response procedures, including notification to affected individuals and authorities where the law requires it.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account or the data you shared with us has been compromised, tell us as soon as you notice it, so we can investigate and contain it.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access: confirm whether we process your data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correction: ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: ask us to erase data, subject to the retention obligations described in section 11.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or have it transmitted to another provider.
  • Restriction and objection: ask us to limit processing, or object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw permission at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Opt out of marketing: unsubscribe from commercial messages using the link in the message or by contacting us.
  • Automated decisions: ask for information about automated decisions that significantly affect you, and request human review where the law provides it.
  • Non-discrimination: exercise your rights without receiving a lower level of service.
  • Complain: lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to exercise your rights

Submit your request to us and we will act on it. We may need to verify your identity first, and we will respond within the period set by the applicable law, normally 30 days. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.

If your data was collected because you paid a merchant that uses WealthPay, we generally act as a processor. In that case we will forward your request to the merchant and assist them in responding.

14. Children's privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16, or under the age defined as a minor by applicable local law. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Third-party websites and services

Our website and dashboard may contain links to third-party websites, plugins or applications. Following those links means the third party may collect data about you. We do not control those services and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal obligations. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with its date at the top. When changes are material, we will notify you by email or through a notice in our dashboard before they take effect. Continuing to use our services after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

17. How to reach us

If you have a question about this policy, about how we handle personal information, or you want to exercise any of the rights described in section 13, send us a request and we will handle it under the terms of this policy.

We aim to resolve every privacy question directly. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the data protection authority of your country or state.
