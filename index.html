How it works

From click to cash,
in four movements.

Collect

Your customer pays through our checkout, your own form or a payment link. Credentials are tokenized before they leave the browser.

Decide

The risk engine scores the transaction in real time and applies your rules, 3-D Secure only where it earns its friction.

Route

We pick the acquirer with the best approval odds and cost for that BIN, and retry elsewhere when a soft decline comes back.

Settle

Funds land in your balance with a full audit trail, then move to your bank account on the schedule you choose.

# Create and capture a payment
curl https://api.wealthpay.io/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WEALTHPAY_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: wpy_90412" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 24900,
    "currency": "usd",
    "capture": true,
    "payment_method": {
      "type": "card",
      "token": "tok_1Nc8y2Kq"
    },
    "statement_descriptor": "WEALTHPAY"
  }'
import WealthPay from "@wealthpay/node";

const wealthpay = new WealthPay(process.env.WEALTHPAY_SECRET_KEY);

const payment = await wealthpay.payments.create({
  amount: 24900,
  currency: "usd",
  capture: true,
  paymentMethod: { type: "card", token: "tok_1Nc8y2Kq" },
  statementDescriptor: "WEALTHPAY"
}, { idempotencyKey: "wpy_90412" });

console.log(payment.status); // "approved"

FAQ

Questions we hear
before every integration.

Most merchants complete onboarding and start processing within 48 hours. Technical integration is usually a single afternoon with our SDKs, and we run the first transactions with you in sandbox before enabling production keys.

Credit and debit cards from the major networks, digital wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, bank transfers, instant payment rails and the local methods your customers expect in each market. Every method settles into the same balance.

Eligible card volume settles on D+1. Other methods follow the schedule of the underlying rail, and you can configure automatic transfers to your bank account daily, weekly or on demand.

If you use our hosted checkout or drop-in components, card data never touches your servers, which keeps your scope at the lightest self-assessment level. If you collect card data directly, we help you map the requirements that apply.

Pricing is per successful transaction, with no setup fee and no monthly minimum. Rates depend on your volume, method mix and risk profile, and we quote them in writing before you sign anything.

You are notified the moment the dispute is opened, with the reason code and the deadline. Evidence is submitted from the dashboard or by API, and the case is tracked to its outcome without you contacting the acquirer directly.
